About Our Church Family

Rooted in Faith, Flourishing in Grace

We are a passionate community of believers committed to preaching holiness, loving unconditionally, and raising champions who reflect Christ in every facet of life.

Our Foundation

A Haven for Spiritual Growth in Sabo, Oyo

The Redeemed Christian Church of God, Abundant Life Area Headquarters is situated at Apata Landlord, Along Oke Mogi, Sabo, Oyo, Oyo State. We exist as a beacon of divine light, hope, and restoration.

Guided by Jesus' promise in John 10:10 ("I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly"), we foster a vibrant worship environment where gifts are discovered, character is shaped, and spiritual breakthrough becomes a daily reality.

Holiness

Our daily lifestyle and ultimate passport to heaven.

Abundance

The Mandate

Our Vision and Mission Statements

Firmly anchored in the worldwide mission of The Redeemed Christian Church of God.

01

To Make Heaven

Our supreme aspiration is eternal salvation and communion with God. Heaven is our ultimate destination and hope.

02

Take Many With Us

We are relentlessly evangelistic—proclaiming Christ’s redeeming love to everyone in our neighbourhood, city, and beyond.

03

Holiness as Lifestyle

To accomplish our heavenly goal, righteousness, integrity, and pure living are actively cultivated in every member.

04

Planting Churches

Taking the gospel within five minutes reach of every soul, establishing thriving fellowships and touching families.

Find Your Place

Ministries at Abundant Life Area

Whatever your age, stage of life, or spiritual gift, there is a place for you to belong, serve, and thrive.

Abundant Life Choir

Ministers of praise leading the congregation into celestial atmospheres of adoration and high praise every service.

Youth & Young Adults (YAYA)

Dynamic, visionary young leaders passionate about purpose, career excellence, spiritual fire, and godly influence.

Good Women Fellowship

Virtuous mothers and sisters praying for their families, supporting the church, and impacting community welfare.

Men of Faith Fellowship

Godly fathers, husbands, and professionals modeling biblical leadership, mentorship, and spiritual strength.

Children's Church

A loving, interactive, and joyful environment where children learn the scriptures through stories, songs, and memory verses.

Media, Sound & Tech

Broadcasting the message of Christ through audiovisuals, photography, livestreams, and community soul-winning campaigns.
