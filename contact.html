First-Time Visitor FAQ

What time should I arrive?

We recommend arriving around 7:50 AM for Sunday School or 8:50 AM for the main Celebration Service.

What about my children?

We have a vibrant and secure Junior Church with dedicated teachers who care for children during service.

What is the dress code?

Come in modest, comfortable, and respectable attire—whether traditional African wear, corporate, or casual smart.
